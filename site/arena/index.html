How to play: your base is on the left, the bot's on the right. Gold arrives slowly on its own; the four piles in the middle hold far more, and any unit standing next to one carries it home by mining (both sides can mine the same pile). Knights (30) are tough and hit close, archers (40) shoot from range. Destroy the enemy base, or hold more base health when the five minutes are up. Keys: 1 knight, 2 archer, A select all, S stop, Space pause, V watch two bots, R new map.
